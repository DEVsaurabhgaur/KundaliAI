# Lal Kitab Principles & Remedial Framework

## Core Mechanics
- Fixed Houses: In Lal Kitab, House 1 is always Aries, House 2 is Taurus, etc.
- **Dharmi Planets**: Benefic protective forces shielding the native.
- **Soi Hue Graha**: Sleeping planets that require specific activation triggers.
- **Qurbani ke Bakre**: Secondary scapegoat planets that absorb malefic blows.
- Non-harmful symbolic remedies acting through psychological and environmental harmony.
