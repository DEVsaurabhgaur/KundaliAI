# Financial Astrology (Vyapar Jyotish) Guide

## Planetary Market Correlation
- **Sun & Jupiter**: Sovereign gold bullion and sovereign bonds.
- **Moon & Venus**: Silver, luxury consumables, and hospitality stocks.
- **Mercury & Rahu**: High-frequency technology, software, and speculative equities.
- **Saturn & Mars**: Industrial commodities, crude oil, and commercial real estate.
