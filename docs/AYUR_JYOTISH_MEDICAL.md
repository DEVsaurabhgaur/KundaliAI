# Ayur-Jyotish & Medical Astrology Specification

## Tridosha Cosmic Correlation
- **Vata (Air & Ether)**: Saturn, Mercury, Rahu | Governs nervous impulses, joints, and breath.
- **Pitta (Fire & Water)**: Sun, Mars, Ketu | Governs metabolic fire, liver enzymes, and vision.
- **Kapha (Water & Earth)**: Moon, Jupiter, Venus | Governs anabolic tissue, mucous membranes, and structural lubrication.
