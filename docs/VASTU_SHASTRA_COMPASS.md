# Vastu Shastra & 16 Energy Zones Guide

## Spatial Energy Zones
- **North-East (Ishanya)**: Supreme cognitive clarity, meditation, and spiritual intake.
- **South-East (Agneya)**: Cash liquidity, metabolic digestion, and electrical drive.
- **South-West (Nairritya)**: Ancestral stability, relationship bonds, and skill mastery.
- **North-West (Vayavya)**: Supportive networking, liquidity, and movement of goods.
