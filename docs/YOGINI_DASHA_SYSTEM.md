# Yogini Dasha System Specification

The **Yogini Dasha** is a high-speed 36-year cyclical timing system particularly celebrated in Northern India for its surgical precision in predicting major events:
- **Mangala (1 Year)** - Moon (Supreme Benefic)
- **Pingala (2 Years)** - Sun (Intense Fire)
- **Dhanya (3 Years)** - Jupiter (Expansion & Wealth)
- **Bhramari (4 Years)** - Mars (Travel & Kinetic Action)
- **Bhadrika (5 Years)** - Mercury (Intellect & Commerce)
- **Ulka (6 Years)** - Saturn (Karmic Trials & Labor)
- **Siddha (7 Years)** - Venus (Sensory Bliss & Creative Triumphs)
- **Sankata (8 Years)** - Rahu (Karmic Surprises & Radical Realignments)
